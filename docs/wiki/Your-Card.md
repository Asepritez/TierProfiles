Style the card other TierProfiles users see when they open yours. Open **Settings → Profile**. The Live Stage on the right shows how it looks.

> Everything here is visual only. It shows on TierProfiles cards and nowhere else. It never changes your real armor, your skin, or anything in the world, and it's free.

## Options

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Background** | Plain, Moon, Starfield, Nebula, Embers, Snow |
| **Border** | Thin, Double, Glow, Shimmer, Corners |
| **Effects** | Off, Sparkles, Motes, Both |
| **Helmet / Chestplate / Leggings / Boots** | None, Leather, Chainmail, Iron, Golden, Diamond, Netherite. Each piece is picked separately |
| **Trims for** | Which piece the trim rows change. **All** changes every piece at once |
| **Trim** | Any trim pattern from the real smithing templates |
| **Trim material** | The colour of the trim |
| **Lists on my card** | Leave a list off your card, for you and everyone else. Handy if you're unranked there |

**Advanced:** Kits on my card (hide kits from your card), Effect strength, Accent (tints your title and border), Featured kit, and Model sways.

For example, to wear a diamond helmet with a netherite chestplate and a gold trim only on the helmet:
1. Pick Diamond in the **Helmet** row and Netherite in the **Chestplate** row.
2. Set **Trims for** to **Helmet**.
3. Pick a **Trim** and the gold **Trim material**.

## How other players see it

When you press **Apply**, your style is saved to the TierProfiles server. Minecraft's usual sign-in check proves it's you, so nobody else can change your card. The status at the top right of the Profile tab says whether it saved.

Other mod users then see your style when they open your card. If someone looked you up shortly before, it can take up to 10 minutes to reach them.

Turning off **Card styles** (Settings → More → Advanced) stops loading other players' styles and saving yours. Your card still looks the way you set it in your own game.
