## Setups

A setup is a complete set of TierProfiles settings: tags, lists, colours, rules, cards and position. You can keep **9**, shown as slots at the bottom of the settings screen.

- **Switch:** press `1` to `9` in settings, or click a slot.
- **Make a new one:** click an empty slot. It starts as a copy of the setup you're on.
- **Rename it or change its icon:** Settings → Sharing.

For example, keep one setup for busy lobbies with only LT3 and up, and another for duels showing everything.

Your own card style isn't part of a setup. It stays the same whichever setup you use.

## Share codes

A share code is a line of text holding one setup, so friends can copy your settings.

**To share:** open Settings → Sharing and press **Copy** next to **Share code**. Send the code to your friend.

**To use a code:**
1. Paste it into **Import** and press **Preview**. The Live Stage shows what it would look like.
2. Under **Include**, choose which parts to take: Filters, Colours, Rules, Card. Anything you leave out stays as it is.
3. Press **Use this code**, then **Apply** to keep it.

Share codes can't carry personal things such as your card style, your privacy answers or the server address. A code from someone else can't change where your game sends anything.
