## Keys

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Tab` + `↑ ↓ ← →` | Pick a player in the tab list |
| `Enter` | Keep the picked player's card open, or close it |
| `Right Shift` | Show **all ranks** on the open card, every kit on every list |
| `F12` | Open TierProfiles settings |

Every key can be changed in **Settings → Keys**. Click a key, then press the new one. `Escape` cancels.

## Opening someone's card

1. Hold `Tab`.
2. Use the arrow keys or the scroll wheel to move to a player. Their row is outlined.
3. While you move, a preview shows. By default it's a small card with their tags. You can change this in **Settings → Cards → Browsing Tab shows**.
4. Press `Enter` to keep the full card. It stays after you let go of `Tab`.
5. Press `Right Shift` to see every rank they have.

The card closes by itself after **Stays open** (15 seconds by default), or press `Enter` again. Set Stays open to 0 to keep it until you close it.

The arrow keys and the scroll wheel only do this while `Tab` is held, so they never get in the way of normal play. The hints under the tab list show the keys while you browse.

## The small card when aiming

Look at a player with your crosshair and a small card with their tiers appears. Turn it off in **Settings → Cards → Tags when aiming**, or change where it sits in **Settings → Position → Advanced**.
