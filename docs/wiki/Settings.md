Press `F12`, or use the button in Mod Menu, to open settings.

## How the settings screen works

- **Nothing changes until you press Apply.** The count beside the buttons shows how many changes are waiting. **Discard** throws them away. After either, a message says what happened.
- **Essentials first.** Each tab shows its main settings. Open **Advanced** at the bottom for the rest.
- **Search everything** with `Ctrl + F`, or click the search bar at the top.
- **Hover any setting** to see what it does. Some choices show a preview of themselves.
- **The Live Stage** on the right previews your changes on your own card, before you apply them.
- **Setups:** the slots at the bottom are 9 complete setups. Press `1` to `9` to swap. See [[Setups and Share Codes]].

## Players

Search any tier-tested player by name. Type at least 3 letters, such as **Hosein**, and every player
whose name has it is listed, best match first. Click one to see their full card on the Live Stage:
their tiers are looked up ahead of anyone else, and their own skin and card style show.

Results come from McPvP's search, which finds part of a name, plus Mojang's lookup for the exact
name you typed, so someone ranked only on another list still turns up if you type their full name.
Players you've already seen show too, marked *Seen before*.

## Tags

Where tier tags show, and what they say. Pick a place with **Show on** at the top. Each place has its own settings:

| Place | On by default |
|---|---|
| Nametag | Yes |
| Tab list | Yes |
| Chat | No |
| Look-at card | Yes |
| Profile card | Yes |

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Show tag** | Turns tags on or off for this place |
| **Minimum tier** | Hides tags below this tier. LT3 is a good choice for busy lobbies |
| **Gamemode** | **Highest** uses their best kit. **Selected** uses your kit list (Lists → Advanced). **Auto** reads what you're holding |
| **Tier** | **Current**, **Peak** (their best ever), or **Both** |
| **Best from any list** | On: shows their highest tier whichever list it's on. Off: shows the first list in your List order that has a tier. On by default |

**Format** (also under Tags): **Tag position** (before or after the name), **Brackets**, **Separator** between tags, and **Long names** (High Tier 3 instead of HT3).

**Advanced:** Hide retired, Hide unranked, Hide my own tag, Apply name rules, Gamemode icon, List logo, Region, and Tags at once (how many lists can show together).

## Lists

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Lists** | Click a list to remove it from tags, names and cards. Click again to bring it back |
| **Kits** | Click a kit to hide it everywhere. Not into SMP? Hide it and it never shows |

**Advanced:**
- **List order:** click a list to move it up. With **Best from any list** off, the first list wins when a tag only has room for one. With it on, list order only breaks ties.
- **Per-list minimums:** a minimum tier for each list, since HT4 means different things on different lists.
- **Selected kits:** the kits the *Selected* gamemode counts.

## Colours

- **Preset:** Default, Heat, Mono, or Colourblind-safe.
- **Tier colours:** click any tier for the full colour picker.
- **Advanced:** how retired tiers look, and separate colours for each list.

## Rules

Rules change how names look, based on tiers. For example, *"anyone HT1 on MCTiers gets a purple bold name"*.

Each rule has a condition (**If tier** at, above, or at-or-above a tier, **On list**, **In kit**) and a look (**Name colour**, **Gradient**, **Bold**, **Star before name**, **Glow around tag**). **Ignore retired** and **Judge by peak** change how the tier is judged.

## Cards

How the profile card and the small aim card behave.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Card detail** | How much each row says: Minimal, Standard or Full. `Right Shift` shows every kit on any card |
| **Last column** | After the third kit: the **date** it was achieved, a **4th tier**, or **nothing** |
| **Browsing Tab shows** | While moving through Tab: **Tags only**, **Full profile**, or **Nothing**. Hover each to preview it |
| **Tags when aiming** | The small card when your crosshair is on a player |
| **Animation** | Glide, Fade, Scale, or Off |
| **Speed** | 100% is normal |
| **Stays open** | Seconds before a kept card glides away, up to 120. 0 keeps it until you close it |

**Advanced:** Peak marks, Retired tiers, Total points, Size (50% to 200%; it shrinks by itself if it would reach the crosshair), and Opacity.

## Profile

How **your own** card looks to you and other mod users. See [[Your Card]].

## Position

Drag the card on the Live Stage, or pick one of 9 **anchors**. **X offset** and **Y offset** fine-tune it, and **Snap** pulls it to anchors and edges while dragging. The card always slides in from its nearest edge.

**Advanced:** where the small aim card sits (below the crosshair, top, bottom left or bottom right), its size, opacity, and a delay before it shows (up to 2 seconds).

## Sharing

Rename the current setup, pick its slot icon, copy its share code, or import someone else's. See [[Setups and Share Codes]].

## Keys

Change any TierProfiles key. Click it, then press the new key. `Escape` cancels.

## More

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Look up tiers online** | Off means nothing is sent and no tiers show |
| **Reduce motion** | Turns off every animation in TierProfiles |
| **Reset all settings** | Puts everything back to a fresh install. Nothing changes until you press Apply |
| **Icon style** | **Default** uses the game's own item textures. **Classic** uses the older Programmer Art look |

**Advanced:**
- **Official list logos:** downloads each list's logo from its own website. Off uses letter marks.
- **Look up from:** **Lists** asks each list's website directly. **Server** asks the TierProfiles server, which asks the lists for you.
- **Card styles:** loads other players' card styles. Off keeps every card plain.
- **Server address:** leave this as it is unless you run your own TierProfiles server.
