## The basics in 30 seconds

1. Install it with **Fabric API**. See [[Installing]].
2. The first time you start the game, a welcome page says what the mod sends. Pick your answers and press **Sounds good**.
3. Join a server. Tiers show next to names straight away.
4. Hold `Tab`, press the **arrow keys** to pick a player, and press `Enter` to keep their card open.
5. Press `F12` to open settings.

TierProfiles is client-side only. Servers don't need it, and it never changes anything in the world.
